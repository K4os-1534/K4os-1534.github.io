Hey, I’m Ismail Shahzad. I’m a cybersecurity student who spends most of my free time breaking websites (legally, of course).

Before security, I was a web developer working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Express.js and Node.js. Building sites taught me how they work. Now I get to learn how they fail. Web exploitation is my favorite CTF category, and my team placed 158th at the Blackhat MEA 2026 CTF.

Right now I’m close to finishing the HTB CPTS pathway and getting ready for the exam. I’m also starting my bug bounty journey, and I’ll write about the wins, the failures and everything in between.

This blog is my personal journal, and it’s also for any student learning cybersecurity. I’ll share machine write-ups, concepts I study in CPTS, and a few random things I find interesting. I’ll keep it in simple words, the way I wish someone had explained it to me.
